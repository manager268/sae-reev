REEV is run like an OEM programme office — three subcommittees reporting through a Convenor to the OC Chair, guided by an Advisory Board of OEMs, Tier-1 suppliers, academia and energy specialists.

Architecture & evaluation

Technical Committee

Owns the REEV 4.0 rulebook, the architecture standard, and the scoring criteria for virtuals, hybrid bench and finals.

Subject-matter experts, by domain

Battery, charging, powertrain, thermal and software subsystems are each mentored by a named industry SME through the season. Full roster to follow closer to kickoff.

Talent pipeline

Alumni Committee

Past REEV participants who now support current teams with hands-on prototyping help, mock evaluations, and the HR/placement conversations that follow finals.

REEV let me put vehicle design on one side and classroom theory on the other, and watch them meet. Getting real industry exposure — two dedicated mentors per team, pushing us to communicate clearly and deliver on time — is something no lecture hall gives you.

— A REEV alumnus, automobile engineering

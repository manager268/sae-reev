Home/Shipping Policy

Last updated: 27 August 2026.

REEV SAEINDIA 4.0 registration is for participation in an engineering competition — it is not an e-commerce purchase, and nothing is physically shipped.

What you get after registering

A confirmed registration (team, individual, judge, mentor, SME, or volunteer) recorded in our system, and — once payment is verified for paid registrations — access to REEV 4.0's technical sessions, virtual reviews, and on-ground finals as described on the Register page.

No physical goods

This policy exists to state plainly, for payment-gateway compliance purposes, that no physical merchandise, kits, or goods are sold or delivered through this site. Any physical items REEV provides (e.g., participant kits, trophies) are handed out in person at the relevant event, not shipped.

What REEV is

An OEM-grade engineering programme, run for students.

REEV — Range Extended Electric Vehicle — is a competition built around range and fuel efficiency. It pushes teams through the same technologies reshaping the auto industry today: hybridization, light-weighting, energy optimization, and range extension.

Students from Computer Science, Electrical, Electronics, Mechanical, Automobile and Production backgrounds form a single cross-functional team and take a vehicle from concept through simulation to a physical build — mentored end-to-end by engineers from the industry.

REEV 4.0

A refined architecture, built on what three editions taught us.

REEV 4.0 is an improvement on the existing platform, not a replacement — aimed at deeper industry relevance.

// PROPULSION

Series-hybrid, always

The electric motor is the sole source of propulsion. The ICE range extender only drives a generator — it never touches the wheels.

// STANDARDIZATION

One motor, one engine spec

A standardized Baja-spec engine and a single motor size replace bespoke generators, widening access and removing ambiguity for new teams.

// ENERGY

Dual battery, software-managed

Two battery packs per vehicle switch under Open VCU software control, activating automatically around 20% state of charge.

// SOFTWARE FIRST

Open VCU is the differentiator

With hardware standardized, teams now compete primarily on control strategy and software — not component sourcing.

// PARTNERSHIP

Built with Baja & e-Baja

Shared engine specification with SAEINDIA's Baja programme means Baja and e-Baja teams can enter REEV with far less retooling.

// MENTORSHIP

Subject-matter experts, by domain

Every subsystem — battery, charging, powertrain, thermal, software — is mentored by a named industry SME through the season.

Event checkpoints

The road from registration to a vehicle on the road.

Every REEV team passes through the same four checkpoints, same as the last three editions.

01

Registration & Technical Sessions

Teams register, then move through simulation workshops on IPG CarMaker, ANSYS and MATLAB.

02

REEV Virtuals

A design-simulation event validating propulsion readiness before any hardware is cut.

03

Hybrid Bench

Teams submit bench video proof of a working hybrid powertrain ahead of vehicle build.

04

REEV Finals

Vehicle readiness and launch — the completed build goes on-road in front of the jury.

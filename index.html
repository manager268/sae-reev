Event Overview

From a single build to a software-first 4th edition.

REEV — Range Extended Electric Vehicle — has run continuously since a 2019 awareness workshop, growing edition on edition into an OEM-style vehicle development competition built around range and fuel efficiency. Three editions in, the 3rd wrapped at GITAM University, Bengaluru with a 2nd Runner-up finish and record participation.

REEV 4.0 marks the biggest shift yet: a standardized engine and motor spec, a dual-battery energy layer under Open VCU software control, and a competition that now rewards control strategy as much as fabrication. Students from Computer Science, Electrical, Electronics, Mechanical, Automobile and Production backgrounds form a single cross-functional team, mentored end-to-end by working industry engineers.

Phase 1 — Virtual REEV 4.0

Design, simulate, defend — before any hardware is cut.

The season opens with 13 confirmed technical sessions across 5 partner organizations — IPG CarMaker and ANSYS simulation, battery systems, electric drives, vehicle safety and more — then moves into REEV Virtuals: teams present their preliminary design and simulation results to a judging panel, defending propulsion readiness and control strategy on paper first.

Ahead of any vehicle build, teams also submit bench video proof of a working hybrid powertrain — the Hybrid Bench checkpoint — confirming the series-hybrid architecture actually runs before it goes on wheels.

Phase 2 — On-Ground Finals

From concept to a vehicle on the road.

Teams bring their completed, fabricated vehicle to the finals venue for safety scrutiny and technical inspection, then a dynamic run in front of the jury — the same on-ground rigor as the last three editions, now judged against the REEV 4.0 architecture standard.

Winners take home cash prizes, a Best Margdarshak (Faculty) award, and — for many — a direct line into HR conversations with the mentoring companies behind the season.

Your REEV 4.0 Journey

Learn → Simulate → Integrate → Build & Prove.

REEV 4.0 is a continuous experiential engineering journey, not a single-day competition.

01

Learn

13 confirmed technical & software sessions across 5 partner organizations — EV/REEV fundamentals, vehicle dynamics, CAD/CAE, battery & powertrain, IPG CarMaker, MATLAB/Simulink, controls, safety and validation. Outcome: the foundation to engineer a REEV. See the full lineup ↗

02

Simulate

Teams convert learning into a virtually validated REEV concept — requirements, architecture, energy calculations, powertrain sizing, CAD/CAE, vehicle simulation, dual-battery strategy, control modelling, DFMEA and design reviews. Outcome: a technically justified vehicle architecture, ready for integration.

03

Integrate

Hardware-software integration of the engine-generator, batteries, BMS, AIRs, charging system, Open VCU and controls — energy flow, switching logic, SoC management, communication, safety interlocks and fault handling, all evaluated on the bench. Outcome: a validated hybrid energy-management system before vehicle integration.

04

Build & Prove

Complete vehicle integration, then static and dynamic evaluation — safety, range extension, efficiency, performance, intelligent energy management, reliability, innovation and technical defence. Outcome: a functional REEV, and proof of the team's engineering capability.

The Engineering Challenge

From Vehicle Integration to Intelligent Energy Management.

REEV 4.0 is a Series-Hybrid Range-Extended EV: the electric motor is the sole source of propulsion, while a standardized engine-generator set tops up a dual-battery energy layer switched automatically under Open VCU software control. With hardware now standardized across teams, the competition rewards control strategy as much as fabrication.

Getting started

How to Participate

StepWhat happens
1. RegisterSubmit your college name and team size between 25 Aug and 10 Sep.
2. Technical sessionsAttend simulation workshops on IPG CarMaker, ANSYS and MATLAB, starting 12 Sep.
3. REEV VirtualsPresent your preliminary design and simulation results to a judging panel.
4. Hybrid BenchSubmit bench video proof of a working hybrid powertrain ahead of the build.
5. REEV FinalsBring the completed vehicle on-road in front of the jury for dynamic and safety evaluation.
